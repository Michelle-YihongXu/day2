5. The long title of the Act indicates that its purposes include detection of people who 
drive motor vehicles after consuming alcohol or drugs, and the creation of offences for 
such people. Section 19 creates an offence where the driver of a motor vehicle on a 
road or road related area has, within the relevant period, the prescribed concentration 
of alcohol in the person’s blood or breath. Section 20 creates a corresponding offence 
where a driver has a prescribed drug in the person’s blood or oral fluid. 3
