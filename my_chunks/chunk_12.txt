12. There are also other reasons for construction of ss 15(1), 15AA and 23 of the Act in the 
manner we favour. Section 15(1) empowers a police officer to “require the person to 
permit a sample of his or her blood to be taken” (emphasis added). Section 23(1) 
applies to a person who “has, in accordance with the provisions specified in this Act, 
been required to permit a sample of blood to be taken for analysis” (emphasis added). In this way, s 15(1) is clearly ‘paired’ with s 23(1).
