The respondent submitted that a nurse or doctor did have the power to 
require a person to provide a sample of blood and that the source of this power lay in 
the application of s 196 of the Legislation Act 2001. 37. The respondent made the following points in aid of her submission:
(a) Section 15 only applied in the circumstances dictated by sub-ss (a), (b) and (c) 
of sub-s 1. Section 23 did not have the same ‘gateway’ provisions so that its 
application could be wider than circumstances arising from s 15. (b) Section 23(1) does not mention s 15; there is no stipulated requirement that 
the offence only arises from circumstances covered by the latter section.
