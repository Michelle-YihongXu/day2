1) A person who – 
a) Has been – 
i. The driver of a motor vehicle on a road or road related area; or 
ii. The driver trainer in a motor vehicle on a road or road related area; and 
b) Has, in accordance with the provisions specified in this Act, been 
required to permit a sample of blood to be taken for analysis;
commits an offence punishable, on conviction, by a maximum fine of 30 
penalty units if the person fails or refuses to permit the sample to be taken 
for that purpose. 2) A person from whom a blood sample is required to be taken under section 
15AA (1) must not behave in such a manner as to make it impossible or 
impracticable for the sample to be taken. Maximum penalty: 30 penalty units.
